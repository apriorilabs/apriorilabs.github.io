Corporate Overview: A Priori Labs LLC is a Huntsville-area, Veteran-Owned engineering and scientific software small business specializing in deterministic real-time digital signal processing (DSP), autonomous interference and anomaly diagnostics, and multi-modal state estimation. Combining first-principles wave physics with disciplined AI-accelerated software engineering, we deliver verified, deployable C++/Python capabilities across radar, sonar/acoustic, and RF sensor systems in weeks—eliminating multi-month study delays and large-contractor overhead.

Core Technical Competencies

1. Deterministic Real-Time DSP & Wavefront Pipelines

  • Low-latency streaming architectures for high-rate IQ baseband, acoustic, and multi-element array feeds on low-SWaP edge compute.
  • Time-frequency & Doppler processing (FFT, STFT, multi-resolution wavelets), pulse compression, and matched filtering.
  • Adaptive beamforming, spatial interference nulling, shallow-water reverberation suppression, and ground/sea clutter mitigation.
  • Constant False Alarm Rate (CA-CFAR, SO-CFAR, OS-CFAR) adaptive detection in non-homogeneous noise environments.

2. Autonomous Signal Integrity, Interference & Anomaly Diagnostics

  • Continuous in-stream monitoring to detect co-channel interference, jamming, multipath distortion, and sensor calibration/phase drift.
  • Automated weak-signature anomaly isolation and transient fault detection before downstream tracking degradation occurs.
  • Empirical sensor pipeline verification: automated derivation of Pd, Pfa, ROC curves, latency jitter, and track-drop root causes.

3. Sensor-Agnostic Multi-Modal Fusion & State Estimation

  • Non-linear Bayesian filtering (EKF, UKF, particle filters) and multi-target probabilistic data association across asynchronous feeds.
  • Modular C++ / Python software engines decoupled from proprietary sensor hardware for rapid COTS and open-architecture integration.

Relevant Domain Experience

  • Radar & Electromagnetic Systems: Doppler target detection, clutter cancellation, kinematic feature extraction, and low-RCS target tracking under degraded SNR.
  • Sonar & Acoustic Sensing: Passive/active acoustic array processing, beamforming, reverberation suppression, and narrowband/broadband contact localization.
  • System Modeling & Verification: End-to-end mathematical pipeline modeling, synthetic clutter/interference injection, and bench-to-field runtime deployment.
